# Notify

The title is one line: what the human needs to decide or see. The body is
optional detail. Attach the material behind it, such as a decision record,
report, or screenshot, rather than pasting it. Send each notice once; routine
progress is not a reason.

Examples and batching uncertain calls: `resources/examples.md`.
When delivery fails: `resources/delivery.md`.
